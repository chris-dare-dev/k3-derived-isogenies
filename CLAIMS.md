# Claims and records

This page maps the results of this repository to the computations that bear on them and to the records of those computations: first the rows of the [guide's Main results](GUIDE.md#main-results), then the computations that belong to none of those rows, then the 47 hypotheses of the ledger of [note 27](docs/27-hypothesis-ledger.md). For each row it names the stored lines that carry its numbers, where single stored lines do, and, in the last column, what the records of this repository's scheduler say about it. Stored results are in the data archive ([DATA.md](DATA.md)) and unpack into `experiments/`.

Evidence labels are those of the notes: **[P]** read in the cited source; **[M]** recalled, not checked; **[D]** derived in this work; **[E]** measured by the scripts in this repository; **[H]** heuristic; **[R]** re-derived in a separate re-derivation check.

## Tasks and records

The tables of the next three sections are generated: their rows are in [claims/claims.toml](claims/claims.toml), and `make tables` writes them here. Every column but the last two is the row as it was written by hand. The cells of the first table are those of the README's table, which is written from the same file. The cells of the ledger table are the columns ID, Statement, Source and Class of note 27, word for word; a test compares them with the note.

**Stored lines** names lines of stored files that carry numbers of the row. When the archive is unpacked, `make check` verifies that each named line holds the text the claim registry expects there. A row without stored lines has its numbers in the notes named in the row, and in the files those notes name.

**Re-run under the scheduler** is computed from the records in [records/](records/). A task is one command of a note's *Reproduce* section, run again in a directory of its own and compared with the stored file it should reproduce ([GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md#running-a-batch)). The column reads:

- **all N tasks ok**, when every task of the row has a record and none differs, with how many were *reproduced* (identical to every stored file they were compared with), *checked* (no stored file to compare with, but the output has the lines it must have) or *ran with nothing to compare* (the output of the original run was not kept), and the day and the commit of the records;
- the count of tasks by status otherwise: `ok`, `mismatch`, `timeout`, `memory`, `error`, `crashed`, `stale` for a record of a task that has changed since it was computed (another command, or other stored files to compare with; such a record supports nothing), and `not re-run` for tasks without a record;
- **not re-run**, when no task of the row has a record. The stored output is then the only support, and it is the original output, not a run of this repository;
- **no task**, with the reason, when nothing in the batch files computes the row.

"ok" is a statement about the tasks, not about the row. A task is the program under check, run again: *reproduced* says that the script, at the recorded commit and on the recorded interpreter, writes what the stored file holds, apart from what the mode of the comparison leaves out (run times, the order of records, and for a few tasks what their caveat states). It is not a second derivation. It says nothing about a statement labelled [P], [M], [D] or [H]: a row that rests on those labels alone has no task, and where a row mixes labels, *Covers* says which part its tasks bear on. [claims/RECORDS.md](claims/RECORDS.md) lists every task of every row with what it is compared with, its record and its caveat.

What the tasks do not cover:

- **The long computations that did not end ok.** [batches/heavy.toml](batches/heavy.toml) holds the computations of a quarter of an hour or more (about 19 hours of recorded wall time in all) and was run once, in 12 h 22 min. Of its 47 tasks 29 are ok and 18 are not (14 mismatch, 2 crashed, 2 timeout); the rows they belong to count them as such, not as ok. [Known differences](#known-differences-between-reruns-and-stored-files) says what is known of each.
- **Statements without a computation.** The first row of the main results, and the hypotheses H29 to H47 of the ledger, which have no section in `sixlines_ledger.py`.
- **The numbers behind the ledger rows, as reruns.** Each of H1 to H28 has the one task `n27/ledger`: the ledger script reads stored files only and recomputes the row's numbers from them, and its output is compared with the stored log byte for byte. Whether the stored files themselves are reproduced is the business of the tasks of the first table. The stored log records 8 of its 170 comparisons as differing from the numbers as first written (note 27 §1); an `ok` of the ledger task says that the log is reproduced, with those 8 lines in it.
- **Computations whose output was not kept.** A task whose verdict is *ran* shows that the command still runs to its end, and nothing more.

## Negative control and limits

Two batches belong to no row above.

- [batches/negative-control.toml](batches/negative-control.toml) holds three tasks whose predictions are wrong on purpose: the ledger and the self-test of the CM method are predicted to differ from their stored logs, and the check of the fourfold over F₁₇ is predicted to print 19 pairs `equal` where it prints 18. Each must end `mismatch`, and the run must end with status 1. If one of them ends `ok`, the comparison of a rerun with its prediction is broken.
- [batches/limits-demo.toml](batches/limits-demo.toml) holds the ledger next to two synthetic tasks that compute nothing: one sleeps past its time limit and must end `timeout`, one allocates past its memory limit and must end `memory`. The run must end with status 3.

Their records are in `records/negative-control` and `records/limits-demo`; a test fails if those records are not the three mismatches, the timeout and the memory stop.

## What the scripts share

The tasks are the scripts of `experiments/` run again, so a rerun shares everything with the stored result it is compared with: it is a check that the result is reproducible, not a check of the result. The harness that runs them (`k3tasks/`, `runners/`, `taskbatch/`) imports no script of `experiments/` and is imported by none; it starts each script as a process and compares files. `tests/test_independence.py` keeps that true.

The checks that the notes label [R] are of three kinds, and they share different things with what they check:

- **The ledger** (`sixlines_ledger.py`, note 27) imports no other script of `experiments/`. It reads stored files only and recounts them. It regenerates none of them: an error in a stored file, or in the script that wrote it, is shared by the ledger's count.
- **The re-derivation checks of notes 18 and 25** (`sixlines_review.py`, `sixlines_review2.py`) import the scripts they check: `sixlines_review.py` imports `sixlines_cm`, `sixlines_dictionary` and `sixlines_prym` and, in the words of its header, "uses the functions of sixlines_prym.py and sixlines_dictionary.py unchanged and adds five checks"; `sixlines_review2.py` imports `sixlines_cmmethod`, `sixlines_delta_labels` and `sixlines_jacobian`, whose "functions are imported and their stored files are read". What is new in each is the check, not the arithmetic underneath it. In the other direction, `sixlines_delta41.py` (note 26) imports `sixlines_review2`.
- **The checks whose findings notes 26 to 29 state** were made with code that is not part of this repository ([docs/README.md](docs/README.md)). Nothing here can say what that code shares.

## Known differences between reruns and stored files

Found by running every batch under the scheduler. The first five are stated in the caveat of their task, and none of them changes a number of the notes. The last two are not resolved.

- **`sixlines_delta41.log` lacks one line.** `sixlines_delta41.py list` prints 32 lines; the stored log has the first 31. Its heading for the torsor at q = 41, e₁ = 22 says 12 surfaces, and it lists surfaces 0 to 10; the line of surface 11 is missing. The task `n26/list` compares the 31 lines and leaves the last line of the rerun out.
- **Most stored records of the coset runs of note 29 predate a key.** `sixlines_order3.py cosets` writes the key `identified_by` in every record since the q = 43 reruns of note 29 §7.4. Of the 18 records of `sixlines_order3_cosets.jsonl`, two have it. For the other runs the key is not compared; every other key is.
- **Two scripts need the fork start method.** `sixlines_fq.py` and `sixlines_nonsplit.py` hand tables to their worker processes by fork. On Python 3.12, the interpreter of the stored results, that is the default. From Python 3.14 the default is forkserver, under which both end with an `AttributeError`; the harness starts them with the fork method set.
- **Logs differ in ways that carry no result**: run times, also inside printed records; the order of the items of a printed dict of counts, which is the order in which worker results arrived; PARI's notice of its stack size. The modes of comparison leave these out ([GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md#what-a-task-does)).
- **The first sweep of note 20 differs, as its caveat says.** The script was corrected after that sweep (note 20 §7). The record of `n20/sweep` is a mismatch in the log and in 24 of the 38 records the rerun wrote; which of them are the records of q = 31 and 37 that the caveat names has not been checked.
